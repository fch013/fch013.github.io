Research is an important part of my life, but it is not the whole story. This page brings together teaching, outreach, and personal interests that do not belong on an academic CV.

## Teaching and outreach

I care about making optics and computational imaging easier to approach through physical intuition, clear mathematical models, and practical demonstrations.

### University teaching

*Teaching assistant · National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University*

From 2016 to 2021, I supported courses in **Electro-Optics** and **Electro-Optical Signal Processing I & II**. This experience shaped how I explain optical systems by connecting physical intuition, mathematical models, and practical implementation.

### Optical imaging demonstrations

I am interested in developing clear, visual demonstrations of light-field imaging, temporal focusing, wavefront sensing, and computational reconstruction. Future materials and events will be documented here as they become available.

### Mentoring and community events

I welcome opportunities to discuss research paths, optical-system development, and the relationship between physics-based models and machine learning with students and early-career researchers. If you are organizing a student event, laboratory demonstration, or public conversation about imaging science, please [get in touch](mailto:tk821017@gmail.com).
