My work connects **optical hardware, forward models, calibration, and computational inference** in one end-to-end imaging process. The goal is to recover useful biological information beyond conventional trade-offs in speed, depth, and resolution.

## Current research

<div class="research-list">
  <article class="research-item" markdown="1">
  <p class="project-meta"><span>COMPUTATIONAL IMAGING</span><span>WORK IN PROGRESS</span></p>
  <h3>Physics-informed deep learning for light-field microscopy</h3>

  I am developing reconstruction methods that embed the physical light-field forward model—including microlens sampling, aberrations, and measurement multiplexing—into learning for robust, high-speed volumetric bioimaging.

  *Related manuscript:* **Richardson-Lucy-Based Deconvolution Network for Light Field Microscopy** (in preparation).
  </article>

  <article class="research-item" markdown="1">
  <p class="project-meta"><span>MULTIPHOTON STIMULATION</span><span>PUBLISHED</span></p>
  <h3>3D computer-generated holography with temporal focusing</h3>

  This work combines temporal-focusing propagation and a digital propagation matrix with learning-based hologram synthesis for rapid 3D multiphoton stimulation and improved axial confinement.

  [Optics Express, 2024](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.505956)
  </article>

  <article class="research-item" markdown="1">
  <p class="project-meta"><span>PHYSICAL MODELING</span><span>WORK IN PROGRESS</span></p>
  <h3>Vectorial temporal-focusing simulation</h3>

  I am developing vectorial spatiotemporal models of DMD-based line-scan temporal focusing to connect illumination geometry, pulse propagation, and system parameters with excitation performance, calibration, and imaging through scattering media.

  *Related manuscript:* **Vectorial Spatiotemporal Modeling of Line-Scanning Temporal Focusing** (in preparation).
  </article>

  <article class="research-item" markdown="1">
  <p class="project-meta"><span>OPTICAL SENSING</span><span>PUBLISHED</span></p>
  <h3>Adaptive optics and wavefront sensing</h3>

  I develop compact sensing and reconstruction methods that embed optical constraints directly into learning, including joint recovery of wavefront and beam intensity from microlens-array measurements.

  [IEEE Photonics Journal, 2025](https://doi.org/10.1109/JPHOT.2025.3561931)
  </article>
</div>

## Research experience

### Massachusetts Institute of Technology

As a visiting scholar in Mechanical Engineering with Prof. Peter T. C. So, I worked on high-speed computational multiphoton imaging through scattering media, including DMD line-scan temporal focusing and silicon-photomultiplier detection.

### National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University

My doctoral research covered light-field microscopy, temporal-focusing multiphoton imaging, computer-generated holography, adaptive optics, and physics-informed image reconstruction.
